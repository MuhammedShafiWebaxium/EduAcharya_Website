What Is B-Tech Credit Transfer?

Academic credit transfer lets an eligible student carry forward accepted credits from a previous institution to a receiving university, subject to its rules and equivalency review.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Understand the basics before you share documents or make an admission decision.

What is inter-university credit transfer?

It is a university-led evaluation of credits earned at one institution for possible acceptance by another institution.

Who is eligible for credit transfer?

Eligibility varies by university. Previous study, passed subjects, curriculum match and available programme rules are normally reviewed.

What documents are required?

Usually mark sheets, transcripts, syllabus or course descriptions, identity documents and the university’s application forms.

Is B.Tech credit transfer legal?

A properly documented transfer completed through a recognised university’s official process can be valid. Avoid promises of instant or improperly shortened degrees.

How many credits can be transferred?

The receiving university decides the limit after comparing your completed subjects with its current curriculum.

Does credit transfer affect employability?

Employment or further-study acceptance depends on the final qualification, university recognition, programme approval and the rules of the employer or institution.
